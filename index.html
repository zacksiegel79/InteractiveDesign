2026's 83rd Golden Globe Awards

By Zack Siegel

83rd Golden Globe Awards ceremony, hosted by Nikki Glaser at the Beverly Hilton, saw a wide range of films and television series honored for excellence in 2025. One Battle After Another emerged as the most celebrated film of the night, winning Best Motion Picture – Musical or Comedy along with awards for Best Director and Best Screenplay for Paul Thomas Anderson, and a supporting performance win for Teyana Taylor. In the drama categories, Hamnet took home Best Motion Picture – Drama, with Jessie Buckley winning Best Actress in a Drama, while Wagner Moura and Rose Byrne scored acting wins in their respective film categories. Additional film honors included Best Original Score for Ludwig Göransson and an animated feature win for Kpop Demon Hunters, highlighting the breadth of creative achievements recognized at the Globes.

On the television side, several shows stood out. The Pitt and The Studio won the top series awards in the Drama and Comedy categories respectively, while Netflix’s Adolescence dominated the limited series field, picking up Best Limited Series plus multiple acting awards for Stephen Graham, Owen Cooper, and Erin Doherty. The night also saw notable television performances from actors such as Noah Wyle and Seth Rogen, reflecting a strong year for serialized storytelling across networks and streaming platforms.

Beyond the competitive categories, the 2026 Golden Globes included historic and first-time wins that marked the evolving landscape of awards recognition. Owen Cooper made history as one of the youngest actors to win in his category for Adolescence, and the ceremony introduced new award categories like Best Podcast, with Amy Poehler’s “Good Hang With Amy Poehler” taking home the inaugural prize.

Lifetime achievement honors also featured:

  • Sarah Jessica Parker receiving the Carol Burnett Award
  • Helen Mirren being celebrated with the Cecil B. DeMille Award
